Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and Apple Pay / Google Pay through our secure Stripe checkout.
Do single exam and bundle purchases expire?
No. Single exam passes and category bundles are one-time purchases with lifetime access. Your questions and score history never expire.
Can I cancel my All Access subscription?
Yes, cancel anytime from your dashboard. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

10 practice questions per exam (110 total across all 11 certifications) with full explanations. No account required.
