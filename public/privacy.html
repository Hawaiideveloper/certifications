1. Scope and contact

This policy describes information handled when you use CertsOnTheFly, create an account, practice questions, purchase access, or contact support. For privacy questions or requests, email support@certsonthefly.com.

2. Information you provide

  • Account information: email address, username, account creation date, and a password hash. The application stores a hash rather than your plain-text password.
  • Practice results: exam category or vendor, question totals, correct answers, score percentage, pass/fail result, time spent, and result date for saved attempts.
  • Purchases: purchased plan, exam or category access, purchase status and dates, and Stripe customer, checkout, and subscription identifiers.
  • Support messages: your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send.

Stripe hosts payment checkout and handles the payment details you enter there. Our application does not store full payment-card numbers.

3. Cookies, browser storage, and usage information

The site uses a session cookie named connect.sid to keep you signed in. Its configured lifetime is up to 30 days. Browser local storage named cotf_free_qs records the number of free questions used on that browser; it remains until cleared.

Pages that load Google Analytics send usage information to Google to help measure site activity. Pages that load Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to display and measure advertising. Service providers may also receive technical information such as IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, and access times when your browser connects to their services.

4. How information is used

Information supports account sign-in, score history and dashboard statistics, paid-access verification, payment and subscription administration, support inquiries, security, troubleshooting, and understanding how the site is used. Advertising helps support the service.

5. Service providers and disclosures

  • Fly.io: hosts the application and its database infrastructure.
  • Stripe: processes payments and subscriptions. We send account email and purchase-related identifiers to connect checkout with your account. Read Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics and AdSense: receive information when their scripts run. See how Google uses information from partner sites and Google's Privacy Policy.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to investigate misuse, protect rights, or secure the service. External links, including affiliate links to study materials, lead to services with their own privacy policies.

6. Advertising and your choices

Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies to show ads based on visits to this site and other sites. Depending on settings and availability, ads may be personalized. You can manage Google's personalized-ad settings at My Ad Center and find participating advertising-industry opt-out tools at YourAdChoices.

You can block or clear cookies and local storage in your browser. Blocking session cookies may prevent sign-in and account features from working. Google also provides a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. These tools have their own scope and may not stop all data collection or all advertising.

7. Retention and security

Account, score, and purchase records are stored to provide ongoing account history and access. The application does not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion schedule to these records. Contact support to request deletion or ask about retention. Some information may need to be retained for payment records, legal obligations, security, or dispute resolution.

The application uses password hashing and session controls, and the production site is configured for HTTPS. No system can guarantee complete security. Avoid sending passwords or full payment-card details in support messages.

8. Privacy requests and international processing

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain uses. Send requests to support@certsonthefly.com. We may need to verify that a request relates to your account. Applicable rights and exceptions depend on your location and the information involved.

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. If you have questions about a provider or a transfer, contact support.

9. Children and policy updates

The service focuses on professional certification preparation and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support so it can be investigated.

We may revise this policy as the site and its practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Contact support if you have questions about a change.
